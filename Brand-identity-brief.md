2. Visual Identity & System Guidelines
2.1 Logo Architecture & Suite
The logo system must be versatile across digital, print, and compact Ui environments.
Please deliver the following core variants:
• Primary Logo (Standard Horizontal): #0364FF (BLUE) #005CFF (ELECTRIC BLUE)
• Secondary Logo (Vertical Stack): #6FA6FF (SOFT BLUE), #F2F4F9 (WHITE)
• Brand Mark / Favicon Icon: A standalone logomark / symbol suitable for mobile app
icons, social profile pictures, and favicons (16x16 to 512x512 px).
• Monochrome & Inverse Variants: Pure single-color versions for high contrast,
embroidery, or monochrome print applications.

readable at large scale.

feature extensive weights (Regular, Medium, Bold, Italic).

3. Design Rules & Guardrails
To preserve brand integrity across external marketing and internal collateral, adhere strictly
to the following usage rules:
3.1 Do's (Approved Usage)
• Always maintain a minimum clear space equal to 50% of the logomark's height around
all sides of the logo.
• Use approved high-contrast combinations for wordmarks and text headers.
BRDGR DESIGNER GUIDELINES
• Ensure icons and illustrations share consistent stroke weight, corner radius, and lighting
style.
3.2 Don'ts (Strictly Prohibited)
• Do NOT stretch, skew, rotate, or distort the logo proportions.
• Do NOT alter logo colors outside the approved brand palette.
• Do NOT place low-contrast logo variants on noisy imagery or complex background
patterns.
• Do NOT add drop shadows, outer glares, gradients, or heavy outlines to flat logo marks
unless explicitly specified in the style guide.
